# sorcerer-spells-2014, part 2 (section-spells-4 … section-spells-9)

Source anomalies, translated as written unless stated:

- `block-section-spells-5-1/item/3` Cone of Cold: source says Fireball does "8d8" (Fireball is 8d6). Translated as 8к8.
- `block-section-spells-7-1/item/6` Fire Storm: source says 7d10 averages 35 (it is 38.5). Translated as written.
- `block-section-spells-5-1/item/13` Skill Empowerment: source writes "Empower Ability" twice where Enhance Ability is clearly meant (no such spell). Rendered as Покращення здібності [Enhance Ability]. Owner may veto and switch to a literal rendering.
- `block-section-spells-4-1/item/8` Ice Storm: source typos "thisis" and "ice Storm's" smoothed in Ukrainian.
- `block-section-spells-5-1/item/16` Telekinesis: source "courches" read as couches (диванів).
- `block-section-spells-4-1/item/14` Storm Sphere: source calls it "Storm Cloud"; kept as in the Wizard page («Штормової хмари»{{Storm Cloud}}).

Wording reused from the finished Wizard / Cleric / Warlock / Druid spell guides when the English matched (Wizard version preferred). Small grammar fixes made against the Wizard text:

- `4-1/item/4` Dimension Door: "вистачає Туманного кроку" (was nominative).
- `5-1/item/11` Insect Plague: "накладання Стіни з каменю" (was nominative); "контролю території" → "контролю області" to match the rest.
- `6-1/item/19` True Seeing: "бачите в Ефірному плані".
- `7-1/item/4` Etherealness: "чуєте Матеріальний план" (Wizard had "чуєте в Матеріальний план").
- `7-1/item/0`, `8-1/item/5`: "заклинач" → "заклинатель" (the usual form in these pages).

Sorcerer = Чародій (catalog), not Чарівник; used in all new text (Sorcerer-specific sentences added to Bigby's Hand, Dominate Beast, Summon Draconic Spirit, Spirit of Death, Tasha's Otherworldly Guise, Sunbeam).
